## 2. Comprehensive GATE General Aptitude — Quantitative Aptitude Reference

### 2.1 Percentages, Profit/Loss, Interest & Mixtures
- **Successive Percentage Changes** of $a\%$ and $b\%$: $\text{Net \%} = a + b + \frac{ab}{100}$.
- **Compound Interest**: $A = P\left(1 + \frac{R}{100n}\right)^{nt}$. For 2 years, $\text{CI} - \text{SI} = P\left(\frac{R}{100}\right)^2$.
- **Alligation Rule**: Ratio of cheaper ($c$) to dearer ($d$) to achieve mean price $m$: $\frac{Q_c}{Q_d} = \frac{d - m}{m - c}$.
- **Repeated Replacement Formula**: Drawing $x$ liters from $V$ liters of pure liquid and replacing with water $n$ times leaves:
  $$\text{Remaining Pure Liquid} = V \left(1 - \frac{x}{V}\right)^n$$

### 2.2 Time, Speed, Distance, Work & Clocks
- **Average Speed** for equal distances at $u$ and $v$: $\frac{2uv}{u+v}$ (Harmonic Mean).
- **Relative Speed**: Same direction $= |u - v|$; Opposite direction $= u + v$.
- **Boats & Streams**: Downstream $= u + v$, Upstream $= u - v$.
- **Work & Pipes**: If $A$ takes $x$ days and $B$ takes $y$ days, together they take $\frac{xy}{x+y}$ days.
- **Clocks**:
  - Angle between hour and minute hands at $H$ hours and $M$ minutes:
    $$\theta = \left| 30H - \frac{11}{2}M \right|^{\circ}$$
  - Hands coincide every $65\frac{5}{11}$ minutes ($11$ times in $12$ hours, $22$ times in $24$ hours).
  - Right angles ($90^{\circ}$): $22$ times in $12$ hours, $44$ times in $24$ hours.

### 2.3 Number System, Algebra, Progressions & Geometry
- **AP**: $T_n = a + (n-1)d$, $S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n-1)d]$.
- **GP**: $T_n = ar^{n-1}$, $S_n = \frac{a(1-r^n)}{1-r}$, $S_\infty = \frac{a}{1-r}$ for $|r| < 1$.
- **AM-GM-HM Inequality** (for positive reals): $\text{AM} \ge \text{GM} \ge \text{HM}$, and $\text{GM}^2 = \text{AM} \cdot \text{HM}$ (for 2 numbers).
- **Number of Divisors of $N = p_1^{a_1} p_2^{a_2} \dots p_k^{a_k}$**: $(a_1+1)(a_2+1)\dots(a_k+1)$.
- **Highest Power of Prime $p$ Dividing $n!$ (Legendre's Formula)**: $E_p(n!) = \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \left\lfloor \frac{n}{p^k} \right\rfloor$.

Important Tips for GATE
1. Master Fundamentals: Ensure a strong grasp of basic arithmetic, algebra, geometry, and data interpretation. Many
complex problems are built upon these foundational concepts.
2. Memorize Formulas: Quantitative Aptitude is heavily formula-driven. Create flashcards or a dedicated notebook for
all key formulas and identities. Regular revision is crucial.
3. Practice Data Interpretation: DI questions are common and can be time-consuming. Practice reading various
charts (bar, pie, line, scatter, radar) and tables accurately and quickly. Pay attention to scales and units.
4. Time Management: Aptitude questions often have quick solutions if the right approach is identified. Don't get stuck
on one question; if it's taking too long, mark it for review and move on.
5. Unit Consistency: Always check and convert units to be consistent within a problem (e.g., km/hr to m/s, cm to
meters) to avoid common calculation errors.
6. Approximation Skills: For questions with close options or complex calculations, develop the ability to estimate and
approximate to quickly narrow down choices.
7. Identify Question Type: Quickly categorize the problem (e.g., P&C, Profit/Loss, Speed-Time) to recall the relevant
formulas and problem-solving strategies.
8. Review Common Pitfalls: Be aware of typical mistakes like sign errors in algebra, confusing permutations with
combinations, or misinterpreting relative speed. Consciously check for these during practice.
