## 2. Comprehensive GATE General Aptitude — Verbal Aptitude Reference

### 2.1 High-Yield Grammar Rules for GATE
- **Subject-Verb Agreement**:
  - Words joined to a singular subject by *along with, as well as, together with, besides, accompanied by* take a **singular verb** (matches the first subject).
  - *Either...or*, *Neither...nor*, *Not only...but also*: Verb agrees with the **nearest (second) subject**.
  - Indefinite pronouns (*Each, Every, Everyone, Someone, Nobody, Neither, Either*) take a **singular verb**.
- **Conditional Clauses (If-Clauses)**:
  - **Zero Conditional** (General truth): `If + Present Simple, Present Simple`.
  - **First Conditional** (Real future possibility): `If + Present Simple, will + V1`.
  - **Second Conditional** (Hypothetical/Unlikely present): `If + Past Simple (were), would + V1`.
  - **Third Conditional** (Counterfactual past): `If + Past Perfect (had + V3), would have + V3`.
- **Subjunctive & "It is high time"**:
  - *It is time / It is high time + subject + **Past Simple (V2)*** (e.g., "It is high time we **left**").

obviously incorrect options first. This increases your chances of selecting the correct answer, especially if negative
marking is less severe.

information given in the statements. Do not bring in outside knowledge or make assumptions. Focus purely on
logical deduction.

agreement, tense shifts, double comparatives, and literal interpretation of idioms. Regularly review these tricky
areas.
